## Illegal content
Ember gives every user tools to protect themselves: block, mute, "not
interested", and shared blocklists from people they trust. The author cannot
remove content from other people's devices. If you come across illegal
content:
- **in France**, report it to the authorities on
  [PHAROS](https://www.internet-signalement.gouv.fr) (or call 17 in an
  emergency); child sexual abuse material can also be reported to
  [Point de Contact](https://www.pointdecontact.net);
- **elsewhere**, report it to your local police or national reporting service.

Block the person in Ember so it stops reaching you through your node.

## Cryptography

cryptology is free (article 30 of loi n° 2004-575, LCEN). Export and use in
other countries may be regulated: check your local law.
